Server-owned captain decision policy

The captain approves an immutable version. The server rechecks its exact authority and source under locks, then commits the answer and evidence together. Coordinators receive read-only attention metadata.

One authoritative evaluator

  • Human plan/explain are read-only. Coordinators hold no captain token.
  • No source, policy generation or budget may change between eligibility and commit.

Activation and cutover are separate

  • Defaults are dry-run; no migration seeds an approved or active policy.
  • Signed human escalation push is absent; retain the existing decision poll until authorized live cutover.

Transaction contract

  1. Creation captures the source after the task’s blocked revision increment and enqueues one generated AshOban worker transactionally.
  2. Apply locks task, decision and active policy state, revalidates mode/approval/source, and samples one database UTC clock.
  3. Shared PR/day and per-decision CI budgets, canonical answer/message/event/wake and immutable ledger commit or roll back together.
